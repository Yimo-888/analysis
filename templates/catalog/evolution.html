Design Evolution — v1 → v2

The first version of this engine was a clean, textbook design: normalize sales, profit and ROI, find the Pareto-optimal frontier, and score on a fixed weighting. It was elegant — and it broke on real operational data. The production version (v2) keeps the spirit but grounds every signal in physical reality. Here's the before/after.

v1 · Pareto frontier

Each point is a SKU in (sales, profit) space. Green points are Pareto-optimal ({{ n_frontier }} of {{ n_total }}) — nothing beats them on all objectives at once.

What reality broke

v1 (idealized)v2 (production)
Sell-through units ÷ current stock → explodes when nearly OOS units ÷ average stock → bounded, honest
OOS items still scored as healthy routed to Slow Mover/Watch & frozen
Scale per-SKU queries bulk-loaded, ~O(1) queries for the whole catalog
Output Pareto / non-Pareto + score 8-way category cascade → lifecycle tier → price action
Missing cost assumed clean fallbacks + guards; feeds the mispricing audit
